GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few useful details before you start your conversation.

Is this a final loan approval?

No. This demo covers initial qualification only. Final eligibility, valuation, interest rate and loan terms are determined by the lender after its review.

What if I need more than ₹75 lakh?

The assistant should explain the maximum amount in this offer and ask whether you would like to continue with that amount.

What if I already have a loan against my property?

The intended flow routes existing LAP or EMI-reduction requests to a loan-transfer specialist rather than treating them as a fresh-loan enquiry.

Will the assistant tell me the exact interest rate?

No. A senior loan expert should provide the applicable interest rate and final terms.
